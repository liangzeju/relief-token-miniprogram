# Catalogue Images

These nine PNGs were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the local demonstration catalogue. They depict supplies, not verified stock or documentary disaster scenes.

Shared prompt: Square catalogue product photograph on a light neutral grey background, complete objects visible with generous margin, crisp details, soft studio lighting, no text, brand or watermark.

- `water.png`: A wrapped case of twelve drinking-water bottles with blue caps.
- `food.png`: Silver emergency-ration packets and generic canned meals.
- `medical.png`: Open first-aid bag with bandages, gauze, gloves and scissors.
- `tent.png`: A pitched emergency shelter tent and folded blankets.
- `rescue.png`: Life jackets, rope, helmet and rescue boat equipment.
- `rebuild.png`: Helmet, shovel, gloves, blocks and toolbox.
- `filter.png`: Portable water-filter pump, tubing and collapsible storage bag.
- `hygiene.png`: Masks, gloves, soap, sanitizer and tissues.
- `bedding.png`: Folded grey blankets and a rolled sleeping mat.

Product specifications, prices and availability come from the backend catalogue, not these illustrative images.
